Connect Google sign-in

A one-time job for you and a trusted adult. The website is built, but Google must recognize it before people can sign in.

  1. Open the Google Auth Platform. Create or choose a project named Give Together.
  2. Complete the setup and Branding details with a trusted adult. For personal accounts, choose an External audience. Keep it in testing while you develop. Add the Google accounts you will test with under Audience → Test users if that option is shown.
  3. Open Clients → Create client. Choose Web application.
  4. Under Authorized JavaScript origins, add http://localhost and http://localhost:3000. Leave redirect URIs empty: this app uses a pop-up sign-in button.
  5. Copy the Client ID. It ends in .apps.googleusercontent.com. You do not need a client secret.
  6. In the Give Together folder, copy .env.example to a file called .env. Make sure it is not named .env.txt. Put the Client ID after GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID=.
  7. Stop and restart START.bat. Open http://localhost:3000 in Chrome or Edge and click Join with Google. Choose a nickname after signing in.
For families and schools

A supervised Google account may need a parent’s approval. A school can restrict which apps students may use. Ask the parent or school to help if Google blocks sign-in. Google sign-in does not verify an organizer’s age or provide adult supervision.

This is still a local prototype. Before inviting children to an online version, work with your adult organizer on who can join, adult supervision, moderation, privacy, and the services’ requirements for children. Do not present this prototype as a fully supervised platform.

Official help: Google setup instructions · Google’s help for children’s accounts

If you previously made a password account, its records remain in the database, but password sign-in is disabled. Google accounts are separate; they are never matched to old accounts by name.
